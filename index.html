Who We Are

About Arcpyx

Boutique IT support and technical recruitment, built for organisations that can't afford to stand still.

Arcpyx was founded on a simple observation: most IT support is reactive, and most recruitment is slow. Both problems have the same root cause — a lack of intelligence at the point of decision. So we built our practice around AI-augmented systems that spot infrastructure issues before they become incidents, and surface the right technical candidate before your best people burn out doing the search themselves.

Today we support 500+ client organisations, from Series A startups to regulated enterprises, and have placed thousands of engineers, architects, and technical leaders across the UK, Ireland, and beyond. Every engagement — support or recruitment — runs through the same principle: human expertise, sharpened by AI, never replaced by it.

We're engineers and technologists ourselves, which means we don't hand you off to a call centre or a keyword-matching algorithm. You get a dedicated team that understands your stack, your culture, and your stakes.
